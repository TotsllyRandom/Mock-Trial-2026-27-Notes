#### Introduction
[[Detective Dana Reyne]] (pronounced "rain") of [[State of Texoma|Texoma Police Dept.]] Homicide joined the unit 6 months ago. This case is her first homicide case to go to trial. She has known [[Charlie Quartz]] and [[Grady Quartz]] since [[May 24, 2025]] due to several incidents regarding [[Grady Quartz]]'s bad behavior and [[Charlie Quartz]]'s bad parenting.

#### Background
[[Detective Dana Reyne]] is single, never been married, and has six nieces and nephews she enjoys spending time with (extremely well behaved). She attributes the good behavior of her nieces and nephews to her family's military background. She joined the Navy in 2004, and served for 6 years, before joining NCIS. She worked there until she moved back to work at the [[State of Texoma|Texoma Police Dept.]] in 2014.

#### Meeting the Quartz Family
Met [[Charlie Quartz|Mr. Quartz]] and [[Grady Quartz|Grady]] on [[May 24, 2025]] because a [[Maurice Whitacre]] (their next door neighbor) had reported his E-bike being vandalized. [[Maurice Whitacre|Whitacre]] was certain [[Grady Quartz|Grady]] was the culprit. 

The front wheel of the bike had been separated, and screws had been removed. [[Maurice Whitacre|Whitacre]] had said he charges at an outlet outside. [[Maurice Whitacre|Whitacre]] had taken his bike to check his corn and had hit a bump, knocking off the front wheel. [[Maurice Whitacre|Whitacre]] called out [[Grady Quartz|Grady]] by name, saying that his wrench was also missing, and accused [[Grady Quartz|Grady]] of shooting down his [[shot down drone|drone]] a few months earlier. Both [[Charlie Quartz]] and [[Grady Quartz|Grady]] denied everything. [[Maurice Whitacre|Whitacre]] never found the drone, and didn't file a report because he believed "no one would believe him without the wreckage."

She then went to the [[Quartz Property]], where she asked [[Charlie Quartz]] about the bike. He denied knowing anything, but brought up the [[shot down drone]]. He said that [[Maurice Whitacre|Whitacre]] wanted to blame [[Grady Quartz|Grady]] for everything from some sort of unexplained hatred.

She then asked to speak to [[Grady Quartz|Grady]], to which [[Charlie Quartz|Quartz]] agreed, albeit in their presence. [[Grady Quartz|Grady]] denied doing anything, but admitted to knowing where [[Maurice Whitacre|Whitacre]] kept his bike at night. Neither Reyne nor Charlie believed what [[Grady Quartz|Grady]] said.
