#### Introduction
[[Detective Dana Reyne]] (pronounced "rain") of [[State of Texoma|Texoma Police Dept.]] Homicide joined the unit 6 months ago. This case is their first homicide case to go to trial. They have known [[Charlie Quartz]] and [[Grady Quartz]] since [[May 24, 2025]] due to several incidents regarding [[Grady Quartz]]'s bad behavior and [[Charlie Quartz]]'s bad parenting.

#### Background
[[Detective Dana Reyne]] is single, never been married, and has six nieces and nephews they enjoy spending time with (extremely well behaved). They attribute the good behavior of their nieces and nephews to their family's military background. They joined the Navy in 2004, and served for 6 years, before joining NCIS. They worked there until they moved back to work at the [[State of Texoma|Texoma Police Dept.]] in 2014.

#### Meeting the Quartz Family
Met [[Charlie Quartz|Mr. Quartz]] and [[Grady Quartz|Grady]] on [[May 24, 2025]] because a [[Maurice Whitacre]] (their next door neighbor) had reported his E-bike being vandalized. [[Maurice Whitacre|Whitacre]] was certain [[Grady Quartz|Grady]] was the culprit. 

The front wheel of the bike had been separated, and screws had been removed. [[Maurice Whitacre|Whitacre]] had said he charges at an outlet outside. [[Maurice Whitacre|Whitacre]] had taken his bike to check his corn and had hit a bump, knocking off the front wheel. [[Maurice Whitacre|Whitacre]] called out [[Grady Quartz|Grady]] by name, saying that his wrench was also missing, and accused [[Grady Quartz|Grady]] of shooting down his [[shot down drone|drone]] a few months earlier. Both [[Charlie Quartz]] and [[Grady Quartz|Grady]] denied everything. [[Maurice Whitacre|Whitacre]] never found the drone, and didn't file a report because he believed "no one would believe him without the wreckage."

They then went to the [[Quartz Property]], where they asked [[Charlie Quartz]] about the bike. They denied knowing anything, but brought up the [[shot down drone]]. [[Charlie Quartz|Charlie]] said that [[Maurice Whitacre|Whitacre]] wanted to blame [[Grady Quartz|Grady]] for everything from some sort of unexplained hatred.

They then asked to speak to [[Grady Quartz|Grady]], to which [[Charlie Quartz|Quartz]] agreed, albeit in their presence. [[Grady Quartz|Grady]] denied doing anything, but admitted to knowing where [[Maurice Whitacre|Whitacre]] kept his bike at night. Neither [[Detective Dana Reyne|Reyne]] nor Charlie believed what [[Grady Quartz|Grady]] said. [[Charlie Quartz|Quartz]] told [[Detective Dana Reyne|Reyne]] that they would punish [[Grady Quartz|Grady]] upon finding proof. [[Detective Dana Reyne|Reyne]] told [[Charlie Quartz|Charlie]] to be on the lookout for [[a lockpicking tool]]. [[Charlie Quartz|Quartz]] said that his son could never sneak something like that in, but would be on the lookout. They also both admitted to knowing that [[Grady Quartz|Grady]] went onto the [[Whitacre Property]] because he knew where [[Maurice Whitacre|Whitacre]] keeps his E-bike.

#### Second Call
[[Detective Dana Reyne]] was called back to the [[Whitacre Property]] on [[June 5, 2025]] at 7 a.m. after [[Maurice Whitacre]] accused [[Grady Quartz]] of throwing purple paint on his [[Whitacre's Car|car]] via a [[paint balloon|balloon]]. [[Whitacre's Car]] was seen unpainted at 10pm the night before. After approaching [[Charlie Quartz|Quartz]], [[Detective Dana Reyne|Reyne]] was told that it was impossible for [[Grady Quartz|Grady]] to have been involved with [[Whitacre's Car]], because [[Charlie Quartz|Quartz]] can see directly into [[Grady Quartz|Grady]]'s room on the [[Quartz Property]], and they were awake the entire night in their living room. 

[[Detective Dana Reyne|Reyne]] pressed [[Charlie Quartz|Quartz]], asking if they had eyes on [[Grady Quartz|Grady]] for the whole night. [[Charlie Quartz|Charlie]] denied, but said they would've known if [[Grady Quartz|Grady]] left the house. There's a [[security system]] in the [[Quartz Property]] that beeps when a door or window is opened. After asking to search the house, [[Charlie Quartz|Charlie]] tried to force [[Detective Dana Reyne|Reyne]] out. [[Detective Dana Reyne|Reyne]] calmed down, and asked [[Charlie Quartz|Quartz]] if there was any paint in the house, to which they confirmed to have (including purple) because they had just repainted their kitchen, which had purple walls that are the same color as the paint on [[Whitacre's Car]]. [[Charlie Quartz|Charlie]] also admitted to having balloons for [[Grady Quartz|Grady]]'s birthday party in January.

[[Grady Quartz|Grady]] was told to get said balloons and brought them to [[Detective Dana Reyne|Reyne]] with a snide remark about the generic nature of the balloons. [[Detective Dana Reyne|Reyne]] said that they saw [[Grady Quartz|Grady]] make a slow smile after the remark and that it "told (them) everything." [[Detective Dana Reyne]] told the Quartzes that there would be no further second chances. They told [[Charlie Quartz|Charlie]] to keep a close eye on [[Grady Quartz|Grady]] since it was clear that he had snuck out in the middle of the night and trespassed on the [[Whitacre Property]].
