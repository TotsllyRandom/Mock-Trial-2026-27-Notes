## Undisputed
---
- [[Julien Younger]] died on [[November 23, 2025]] as a result of [[Clartaxin]] poisoning. 

- [[Charlie Quartz]] was convicted of [[Negligent Homicide for Failure to Control a Minor]] on [[November 23, 2025]].

- Samples of residue were found on [[Charlie Quartz|The Defendant]]'s table in his basement during the execution of a search warrant on [[November 25, 2025]]. The residue tested positive for [[Clartaxin]].

- Other than the samples of residue, no other illegal or unlawful things were found on the [[Quartz Property]].

- [[Maurice Whitacre]] was found dead inside his home on [[December 25, 2025]] by police officers preforming a welfare check after [[Maurice Whitacre|Whitacre]] failed to show at a family Christmas dinner. There was no evidence of foul play or forced entry.

- [[Maurice Whitacre]]'s Medical Examiner's initial conclusion at the scene was that [[Maurice Whitacre|Whitacre]] had died of a heart attack 4-6 weeks earlier. No autopsy was preformed.

- [[Grady Quartz]] never possessed any illegal drugs or illicit substances, including alcohol or recreational drugs, at any time.
