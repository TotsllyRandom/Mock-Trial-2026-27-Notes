Exhibit 11 is an International Travel and Document Encounter Summary created by the United States Department of Homeland Security, Customs and Border Protection (“CBP”), and provided to the Texoma Police Department on [[November 27, 2025]]. CBP has access to real-time data regarding all United States passports encountered at all international customs and border control checkpoints in Europe, and the parties agree that the information reflected in Exhibit 11 is the only data available regarding the passports of [[Morgan Snyder]] and [[Grady Quartz]] between [[November 1, 2025]], and the day of trial. Exhibit 11 is admitted.
